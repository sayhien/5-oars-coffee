Our Story

One shophouse on Tanjong Pagar Road, a move east, and a return to where it started.

We are a local homegrown cafe that believes in connecting the community through our signature brunch classics and freshly roasted coffee.

Five Oars began on Tanjong Pagar Road as one of the first minimalist cafes in the neighbourhood, moved east to Upper East Coast, and returned to Tanjong Pagar as Heritage, a Melbourne-inspired room that runs from morning coffee through to a night menu.

Here at Five Oars, we pride ourselves on using high quality ingredients & making our food from scratch. From housemade breads & pastas to our sauces, we strive to ensure that we only serve the best.

As the story goes, we were just friends in a living room, raving about the simple satisfaction that great coffee brings and Five Oars Coffee Roasters was born.

We sought to discover flavours and aromas of life hidden in bean varietals all over the world, and make them known to the world back home. Delicious food and coffee, coupled with unforgettable service can catch you up in the weekend you so crave and deserve — We know this from experience.

But we needed to carve out a place of respite in our concrete jungle to practice and enjoy our craft with those who appreciate it most. That sense of comfort in community is our anchor.

We hope you would join us on this leg of the Five Oars Coffee Journey as we take our craftsmanship up a notch. Step on board and sail into the endless weekend with the ones you call home.

Where the beans come from

Coffee that carries a name, a valley and a season

Every lot on this page came from a farm we buy from directly, season after season. Pharn picks 50 to 70 kilograms of cherries a day in Laos. In Nyeri, the Kagumo washing station dries its naturals on raised beds for three weeks. We pay for that care, and you can taste where it went.

Roasting happens in small batches at Upper East Coast, cupped on the same bar that serves it. Pick a lot and follow it through the fortnight, or ask the bar which one is drinking best today.

Vixay Huaywaiy, six hectares

Vixay, 55, has been growing coffee on his six-hectare farm for over two decades, managing four separate plots. As a member of our cooperative, he sells coffee parchment to us while also working with other buyers to diversify his income. Despite challenges like pests and weather, Vixay continues to adapt and invest in his farm, with support from his family and the cooperative.

Front of House

Part-time and Full-time positions available.

+

Our front of house crew represents the service and overall hospitality of the café. You should provide consistently excellent customer service and ensure are comfortable and satisfied throughout the visit. The position requires someone who is a team player, service orientated, and passionate about the industry.

Responsibilities

  • Offer friendly and efficient service to customers
  • Provide menu recommendations to customers when asked
  • Process customer orders via Point-of-Sale (POS) system
  • Ensure all items are prepared and served promptly
  • Assist with setting and clearing table for various meal period
  • Assist shift manager in planning and organizing special events
  • Escalate customers' feedback to the shift manager
  • Follow proper hygiene practices in accordance with food & beverage safety and hygiene standards
  • Any other duties assigned by the manager

Requirements

  • At least 1-2 years of relevant experience in the F&B or Retail industry
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  • Excellent team player and friendly individual
  • High initiative and positive attitude
  • Able to multi-task and work in a fast-paced environment
  • Willing to work on weekends and public holidays
  • 5 days work week (for full-time positions)
